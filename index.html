Clinical Notice: Standardized risk probabilities calculated via 2024 AHA PREVENT™ and 2013 ACC/AHA PCE equations.

Modified ADA Diabetes Risk Estimator: The baseline American Diabetes Association (ADA) screening tool has been modified in this sandbox to incorporate additional cardiometabolic risk factors not originally included in the ADA calculator. Specifically, we have added assessments for Tobacco Use (Current or Former Smoker) and Dyslipidemia (Total Cholesterol > 200 mg/dL or HDL < 40 mg/dL). These factors were integrated based on well-established clinical research indicating their significant contribution to overall cardiometabolic and diabetes risk, thereby improving the predictive accuracy of the score for our population.
